# Ƙirar Model (Models a Harshen Hausa)

Cikakken bayani kan ƙirar fasahohin hankali na wucin-gadi da ke cikin tsarin N-ATLaS.

---

## 1. Ƙirar Harshe Mai Faɗi (LLM): `NCAIR1/N-ATLaS`
* **Girman Ƙira**: Ma'auni biliyan 8.03 (Llama-3-8B)
* **Harsunan da ta ƙware**: Hausa, Yarbanci, Inyamuranci (Igbo), Turancin Najeriya (Nigerian English), da Turancin Pidgin.
* **Bambanci na musamman**: Tana fahimtar karin magana, al'adu, da kuma salon maganar mutanen Najeriya dalla-dalla.

---

## 2. Ƙirar Fahimtar Murya (ASR Models)
* **Hausa**: `NCAIR1/Hausa-ASR` (Fiye da awanni 120 na sautin asali)
* **Yorùbá**: `NCAIR1/Yoruba-ASR` (Fiye da awanni 627 na sautin asali)
* **Igbo**: `NCAIR1/Igbo-ASR` (Fiye da awanni 120 na sautin asali)
* **Nigerian English**: `NCAIR1/NigerianAccentedEnglish` (Fiye da awanni 120 na sautin asali)
